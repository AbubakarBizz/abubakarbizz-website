Law Offices of Ramin Ghashghaei
Why I reached outYour firm's experience and community connection are already clear. I noticed a possible opportunity in what happens after someone first contacts you.

I spent time reviewing your website, practice areas, contact page, and team information. A couple of details stood out, especially the way your firm combines long-standing immigration experience with a Spanish-speaking team and two Los Angeles-area offices.

01 / What stood out

A strong story, backed by real experience.

Your website speaks about immigration work dating back to 1987. It also introduces team members with years of experience supporting clients, including Spanish-speaking clients. That combination of legal experience and personal understanding is a meaningful point of difference.

02 / An opportunity to explore

Make the first inquiry easier to route.

Your contact page appears to start with a general inquiry form. A more guided first step could ask which type of matter the person needs help with, their preferred language, and which office is convenient. The inquiry could then reach the right team member, followed by a clear next step toward a consultation.

What I would look at first

1. Guide the inquiry

Give visitors a simple way to identify their matter type, preferred language, and preferred office.

2. Route it clearly

Make sure each inquiry reaches the appropriate person, with a clear owner for the next response.

3. Follow through

Track whether inquiries become booked consultations, and create a consistent follow-up for people who do not book right away.

03 / A question for you

When a new inquiry comes through your website, is it already routed by case type and preferred language, or does your team handle that manually?
